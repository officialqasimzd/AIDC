# Provisional problem statement

Use the same four questions from the lecture, then add the two that follow.

- **User:** The plant operations manager and maintenance supervisor will act on the result.
- **Decision:** They may change which production line is inspected first or scheduled for maintenance.
- **Evidence:** Available evidence includes line-level records for units produced, downtime, rejected units, energy use, timestamps, shifts, and equipment conditions.
- **Important risk:** If the assessment is wrong, maintenance time may be spent on the wrong line while a genuinely problematic line continues to lose production or create rejects.
- **Intended value:** Better-prioritised interventions should reduce avoidable downtime and rejected units without unnecessarily disrupting production.
- **Assumption to check:** The recorded production, downtime, reject, energy, and equipment measurements are complete, accurate, and comparable across lines and shifts.
